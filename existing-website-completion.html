Existing website completion

Keep what works. Finish what matters next.

Evolved Designs designs and maintains websites. If you already have a live site or a partly completed build, we can scope a focused completion phase instead of assuming a rebuild.

Bring three facts

  1. Your current platform. Name the website builder, content system, store, booking tool, or custom application already in use.
  2. What must keep working. List the pages, forms, payments, bookings, accounts, automations, or integrations that should survive the work.
  3. The next customer action. Choose one action the next phase should support, such as requesting service, booking, buying, paying, or signing in.

Public URLs are enough for an initial conversation. Keep passwords, customer records, payment details, and private system data out of the first note.
