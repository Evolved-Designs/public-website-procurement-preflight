Why these decisions appear here

Current federal accessibility acquisition guidance treats accessibility as a procurement-lifecycle requirement: define it before release, request evidence from vendors, evaluate proposals, and validate contractor compliance after award. The DOJ's current state and local government guidance identifies WCAG 2.1 Level AA as the technical standard for covered web content and mobile apps.

  • ADA.gov: state and local government web accessibility compliance guide
  • Section508.gov: buy accessible products and services
  • Section508.gov: request accessibility information from vendors
  • City of Excelsior Springs: current municipal website RFP
  • City of Excelsior Springs: current proposal and contract addendum

This planning aid is not legal advice, a compliance certification, or a substitute for counsel, an accessibility program manager, or independent acceptance testing.
