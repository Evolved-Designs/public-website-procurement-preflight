# Business Pitch-to-Website Proof Card

Updated September 2, 2026

A public pitch can create a burst of attention before a small business's website is ready to prove the same story. Use this card before a pitch final, expansion-grant review, launch event, or funding announcement to connect each public claim to current first-party evidence and a working visitor path.

This is a planning aid, not a promise of funding, traffic, sales, or conversion results. Test with synthetic information unless the business has approved a protected-data procedure.

## Start with one public claim register

Record only claims the business owner or an assigned source owner can approve. Do not copy an unverified directory, social caption, old flyer, or AI-generated summary into the website as fact.

**Claim parity rate = matching applicable claims / applicable claims x 100**

Do not average away a material conflict. A wrong location, date, price, eligibility statement, or primary action remains a release blocker even when the overall rate is high.

## Build the smallest defensible proof path

For each important claim, give a visitor a direct first-party route to evidence or action:

1. Put a plain-language value statement and current offer on the landing page.
2. Link proof to its real source: a dated case summary, approved testimonial, product detail, credential, public record, or owner-approved milestone.
3. Put one primary action beside the claim it supports.
4. Explain the next step, response window, price boundary, or eligibility boundary before collecting information.
5. Keep the destination on a business-controlled or approved operational system; label third-party handoffs clearly.
6. Remove or qualify claims that cannot be substantiated now.

Do not call a click, form start, registration, RSVP, waitlist entry, or payment handoff a completed sale. Record the deepest event the business can actually verify.

## Test three visitor journeys

Use synthetic names, email addresses, phone numbers, and order details. Do not submit a real application, purchase, reservation, membership, medical form, financial form, or SMS consent during a readiness test.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| Understand the offer and find proof |  |  |  | Claim and source are visible and consistent | pass / fail |  |

**Decision-path completion rate = passed applicable journey-device checks / applicable journey-device checks x 100**

Record the page URL, test time, viewport, destination, observed result, and evidence reference for every failure. A click alone is not an outcome.

## Pass six release gates

- [ ] **Claim gate:** an assigned owner approved every material claim and its source date.
- [ ] **Parity gate:** no material website, profile, directory, pitch, or operational-destination conflict remains.
- [ ] **Action gate:** the primary path works on mobile, desktop, and keyboard without a misleading result label.
- [ ] **Data gate:** the form collects only necessary information, shows the next step, and follows the business's approved privacy procedure.
- [ ] **Evidence gate:** every published result, credential, testimonial, or comparison has a retained source and permission where required.
- [ ] **Recovery gate:** the team recorded the before state, approver, publish time, monitoring owner, and rollback action.

Publish only after all applicable gates pass. If an item is intentionally deferred, name the owner, the return time, and the exact wake condition.

## Use the 72-hour pitch window

### Before the pitch

- Freeze the approved ten-claim register and archive screenshots or exports of the current surfaces.
- Choose one landing URL and one primary action; create a tagged link for the pitch and event materials.
- Run the synthetic journey matrix and correct material conflicts first.
- Prepare a short fallback contact route in case the primary destination fails.

### During the attention window

- Monitor availability and error signals; do not make unsupported claims to chase traffic.
- Record visits, primary-action starts, valid completions, and qualified responses as separate measures.
- Capture questions people actually ask and assign each reusable question to a page owner.

### After the pitch

- Reconcile public statements with the claim register before posting a recap.
- Review failed or abandoned paths without retaining unnecessary personal data.
- Keep useful corrections; revert speculative changes that lack evidence.
- Set a dated review for time-bound offers, launch language, finalist or award claims, and temporary hours.
