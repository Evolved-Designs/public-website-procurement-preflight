# Website Goals Conversation Starter

A website conversation is more useful when it starts with the business goal, not a long feature list. Use this short brief to prepare for a focused first conversation with a web partner.

## Bring four things

- One public website or page, if you have one.
- The business change behind the conversation: a launch, a new offer, a move, a growth goal, or a process that needs to work better.
- The one action you most want a visitor to complete.
- Any working tool or process that should stay in place.

Keep credentials, customer information, private analytics, and confidential plans out of the brief.

## Finish these sentences

- **Today, our website helps us:**
- **Next, we need it to help us:**
- **The visitor who matters most should be able to:**
- **Our team will know the next step worked when:**
- **The first deadline or decision date is:**
- **The system, content, or relationship we need to protect is:**

## Shape the smallest useful first phase

Ask the web partner to explain:

1. What can be learned or improved in one bounded first phase?
2. What will stay unchanged while that work happens?
3. What decision, content, or access is needed from your team?
4. What evidence will show that the phase is complete?
5. What should happen next if the result is useful—or if it is not?

You do not need a complete technical specification before the first conversation. A clear goal, one visitor action, and an honest timing or budget boundary are enough to begin.
