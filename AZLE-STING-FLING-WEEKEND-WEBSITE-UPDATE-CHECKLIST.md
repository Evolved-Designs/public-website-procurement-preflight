# Azle Sting Fling Weekend Website Update Checklist

Azle's 2026 Sting Fling vendor festival is scheduled for **Saturday, September 12, from 9:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.** on **Azle Main Street and at 404 Main Place**. The event calendar also lists a parade and classic car show that morning.

This short checklist is for an Azle business or community organization making a focused website update before or after the event. It does not require a redesign.

## Before publishing an event-week update

- Write down the one action visitors should take: visit, call, order, reserve, buy a ticket, or request information.
- Confirm the date, time, location, parking note, and contact route against the event organizer's current page.
- Put the most important weekend information where a phone visitor can find it without opening several menus.
- If regular hours are changing, state both the event-week hours and when normal hours return.
- Keep one clear link or button for the intended action. Use specific text such as **View event hours**, **Order online**, or **Ask about availability**.
- Preserve any booking, ordering, donation, or ticket system that already works. Test the handoff without creating a real order or appointment.
- Check the page with keyboard-only navigation and at a narrow mobile width. Confirm that links, buttons, labels, and error messages remain usable.
- Share the same approved details on the website and any public listing or social post you plan to use.

## Keep the update bounded

A useful event-week change may be a banner, hours notice, menu or service update, event page, booking link, or contact route. Do not add claims about availability, discounts, parking, sponsorship, attendance, or event access unless the responsible source has approved them.

Use synthetic test details only. Do not place customer information, passwords, private analytics, or unpublished business information in public pages or test submissions.

## Plan the after-event change now

Before publishing, name the person responsible for removing or revising temporary information. Record:

- the page or section changed;
- the approved source;
- the publish time;
- the after-event owner;
- the date normal content should return; and
- the smallest rollback if the update causes a problem.

After the event, test the main visitor action again and remove expired banners, hours, or registration language. Record provider acceptance and a real inquiry separately. A page view or click is not a lead, booking, or sale.
